Generate Assertion and Reason Questions (1 Mark each) for CBSE Class 10 Science.

REQUIREMENTS:
- The `stem` MUST contain:
  "Assertion (A): [Statement]"
  "Reason (R): [Statement]"
- The `options` MUST be EXACTLY the standard 4 CBSE choices:
  [
    "Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).",
    "Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).",
    "Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false.",
    "Assertion (A) is false but Reason (R) is true."
  ]
- `correct_option` must be "A", "B", "C", or "D".
- The `answer` must state the correct option letter and clearly explain why Reason either explains or does not explain Assertion.
- The `marking_scheme` has 1 step with 1 mark.
